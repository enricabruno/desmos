DeSMòS (Descriptive Semantic Model for Structured Texts)
description
DeSMòS (Descriptive Semantic Model for Structured Texts) is an OWL 2 ontology for the formal representation of literary constraints as text-generative procedures, with particular reference to the Oulipian tradition. The model treats constraints as conceptual entities distinct from works and their bibliographic realisations, represented through LRMoo, and describes their use in expression creation events, distinguishing the procedure from both the creative process and the resulting text. Constrained rewriting is represented through derivation and intertextual relations between expressions, allowing the identification of the textual passages involved. The typological characterisation distinguishes formal and semantic constraints, with a specific category for visual constraints, and describes the constrained units, procedural operations, and origins through SKOS controlled vocabularies. The model also supports the documentation of constraint use through intratextual evidence and paratextual statements, whether peritextual or epitextual, distinguishing responsibility for such statements from responsibility for textual creation. Through the reuse of and alignment with CIDOC CRM, LRMoo, PROV-O, SKOS, INTRO, Dublin Core Terms, and Schema.org, DeSMòS supports the description, comparative analysis, and SPARQL querying of constrained writing practices across heterogeneous corpora.

comment
In DeSMòS, INT31 Intertextual Relation is used to represent an intertextual relation between texts or their passages as an independent entity, specifying the entities involved and the relation type. The properties that can be used with this class are intro:R12_hasReferredToEntity, intro:R13_hasReferringEntity and intro:R19_hasType.
